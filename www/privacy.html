Tr.emoji Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Tr.emoji is a language-learning application created by Paul Delaquis. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Tr.emoji iOS application.

Information Collected

Tr.emoji does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect names, email addresses, contact information, or other identifying information through the app.

Tr.emoji does not sell personal information and does not use personal information for advertising or tracking.

Microphone and Speech Recognition

Some activities allow users to answer by saying a word or phrase aloud. When you choose to use these features, Tr.emoji requests permission to access your device’s microphone and speech recognition services.

Speech input is used to determine whether the user has spoken the expected word or phrase and to provide the corresponding result within the game. Tr.emoji does not store audio recordings or create a personal voice profile.

Speech recognition is provided through services made available by the device’s operating system. The processing of speech input by those services is governed by the applicable privacy practices of the service provider.

You may deny or revoke microphone and speech recognition permissions through your device settings. Other parts of Tr.emoji can still be used without these permissions, although activities that require spoken answers will not function.

Information Stored on Your Device

Tr.emoji stores certain information locally on your device, including preferences, language settings, progress, and game scores. This information is used to preserve your settings and app experience.

This locally stored information is not transmitted to the developer. It can be removed by deleting the app or by clearing its stored data where supported.

Third-Party and System Services

Tr.emoji uses system features provided by Apple, including text-to-speech, microphone access, and speech recognition.

Tr.emoji does not include third-party advertising networks and does not use information for cross-app tracking.

Data Sharing

Tr.emoji does not sell, rent, or share users’ personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children’s Privacy

Tr.emoji does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account or request personal details from users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Tr.emoji’s features or privacy practices change. Any revisions will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
